What this site collects

1. Server and hosting logs

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records requests it serves, which can include your IP address and browser user agent. This happens for every visit and is necessary to deliver the page and keep the service secure and available. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in delivering and securing the site (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

2. Web fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com and those servers receive your IP address. No cookies are set by these requests.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in presenting the site consistently (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

3. Analytics — only with your consent

If you accept analytics, the site loads Google Analytics 4 and Contentsquare. These record how the guide is used — pages viewed, filters selected, how far down the page readers scroll — so the guide can be improved. They set cookies on your device and share data with providers outside the EU.

If you decline, or simply ignore the banner, neither script is loaded and neither sets a cookie. Nothing about analytics runs before you choose. Google Consent Mode is set to "denied" by default on every page load.

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the ePrivacy Directive). You can withdraw it at any time — see below.

Who your data is shared with

The site sells nothing and shares nothing for marketing. Data reaches only the service providers needed to run it:

  • GitHub, Inc. — hosting (always).
  • Google (Fonts) — typefaces (always).
  • Google (Analytics 4) — usage statistics (only with consent).
  • Contentsquare SAS — usage analytics (only with consent).

Google and GitHub are based in the United States and may process data there under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Links to parking operators

The guide links to parking operators, the airport, and transport providers. Once you follow a link you are on that company's site, under their privacy policy, not this one. This site receives no commission data or booking details back from them.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or EEA you have the right to access, rectify or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out. Write to contact@airportparkingbudapest.info to exercise any of these.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In Hungary this is the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), naih.hu.

Children

This site is aimed at adults planning airport parking. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts using a new service that processes personal data, this page is updated and the date at the top changes. Material changes to analytics will ask for consent again.
