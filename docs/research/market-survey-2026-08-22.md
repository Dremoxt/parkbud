## 2. Quick read on the market

- **Cheapest 1 day:** Hotel Ferihegy Parkoló 4,990 · Park & Bark 5,800 promo · Star Park 5,900 · MoPark 5,990 · NOA 5,998 · ABC Gold / myBUD 6,000.
- **Cheapest 7 days:** ABC Gold / myBUD 11,500 · Park & Bark 11,800 · NOA 11,992 · Ferihegy Top / Airport Parking Hungary / Safe Parking 12,000.
- **Official vs off-airport crossover** is at roughly **day 2**. One day at official Smart Parking (6,700) is competitive; seven days official Smart (~16,100 drive-up) vs ~11,500–14,000 off-airport, and at 14 days the gap runs to tens of thousands of forints.
- **Structural pricing quirk:** most off-airport lots charge a high first day (6,000–9,900) then 700–1,000 Ft per additional day. This makes them poor value for a 1-night trip and excellent for 7–24 days. Európa Parkoló's flat 24,000 for 12–24 days (~1,700 Ft/day) and Budapest Reptér Parkoló's +1,000/day are the clearest examples.
- **Differentiators worth noting:** indoor/covered bays (BUDCAR, Parkolómester, Nemzeti, Park & Bark, MoPark, Comfort, FAP, Best Parking); EV charging (ABC Gold ~200 Ft/kWh, Európa 120 Ft/kWh, Parkolómester); car wash/valeting (BUDCAR, Best Parking, Union, Park & Bark, Nemzeti, plus official Park 'n Wash); dog boarding (Park & Bark — unique); departure-level drop-off rather than arrivals level (Budapest Reptér Parkoló, myBUD); oversize/bus/motorhome storage (Best Parking, Nemzeti).
- **Language reality:** the single biggest gap in this market. Only **BUDCAR (8 languages)** and **Best Parking (6, incl. Chinese and Korean)** go meaningfully beyond HU/EN. Roughly a third of the lots — including several with 500+ reviews — are **Hungarian-only**, which reviewers on Ferihegy Top explicitly complain about. The comparison layer (ParkoLow, airportparkingbudapest.info) is multilingual where the operators are not.

## 3. Comparison with airportparkingbudapest.info

**What the site is:** "ParkBUD", an independent, non-affiliated editorial directory. Served in **7 languages (EN, HU, DE, RO, SR, HR, SK)** — better language coverage than almost any individual operator. It carries a "**33 · Prices checked 21 Aug 2026**" badge, filter chips for type / walking vs shuttle / 24-7 / EV charging / car wash / covered, plus sections on nearby services, directions, city transport and driver tips. It has no booking engine; every listing is an outbound link.

### Where it agrees with my findings

| Point | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Five official on-airport zones (Premium, Terminal, City Break, Holiday, Smart) with walk times 1 / 2–4 / 2–5 / 4–9 / 8–10 min | ✅ Matches |
| Off-airport model: fenced, camera-monitored lots in Vecsés with free 24/7 shuttle, 5–10 min | ✅ Matches |
| City-transport figures (taxi ~9,000–12,000; miniBUD ~3,500–5,500; 100E 2,200; 200E+M3 900) | ✅ Consistent with current BKK/Főtaxi pricing |
| Individual operator descriptions (BUDCAR stone wall + garages + car wash; Best Parking terrace/washroom/child seat; Parkoló Mester VIP transfer + EV charging; Budapest Reptér Parkoló roadside assistance) | ✅ All confirmed against the operators' own sites |
| "Book official parking at least 6 hours ahead" | ✅ Matches parkolo.bud.hu's stated rule |

### Where it falls short

**1. The prices are not actually retrievable.** The headline claim is "from 1,600 Ft/day" and a "33 prices checked 21 Aug 2026" badge, but the per-lot price values are injected client-side — **not a single price appears in the served HTML for any of the 33 listings**, in either the EN or HU version. Nothing on the page can be price-verified by a crawler, a citation checker, or a user with JS disabled. By contrast ParkoLow publishes a full 1–10 day price matrix in-page with a per-lot "confirmed on" date. Separately, "**from 1,600 Ft/day**" does not correspond to any real entry-level price I could find: the cheapest single day at BUD is 4,990 Ft, and the lowest *marginal* daily rates (700–1,000 Ft/day) only apply from day 2 onward. The figure appears to be a marginal rate presented as an entry price.

**2. Addresses are largely absent.** Only 7 of 33 entries carry any location detail, and those are street-level at best ("Vecsés, Széchenyi u."). Twenty-plus entries say "~3 km from airport (est.)". No house numbers, no GPS, no phone numbers anywhere on the page — the three things a driver actually needs.

**3. The distance figures are unreliable.** The blanket "~3 km (est.)" is wrong for a large share of the list. Measured against ParkoLow's per-lot distances: NOA is 5.4 km, Dobro 6.2 km, Hungária 5.2 km, Nemzeti 6.3 km, Zoka 5.0 km, Repteres 4.8 km, Sky 3.0 km, Park Central 3.7 km. Its two *specific* off-airport figures are also low: BUDCAR listed at 2 km (measured 3.7 km — the site appears to have copied BUDCAR's own marketing claim) and Comfort Parkoló at 2 km (measured 4.1 km).

**4. Sixth official car park is missing.** The **Arrivals pick-up area** is omitted. It carries the steepest tariff on the airport (10,000 Ft for 2 h, 50,000 Ft for 10–24 h) and is exactly the zone an uninformed driver blunders into while waiting for a delayed flight — the single most expensive mistake the guide could have warned about.

**5. Probable duplicates and one likely address error.** "Ferihegy Airport Parking" (linked to repterparkolo.hu) and "FAP" (linked to fedettrepterparkolo.hu) look like the same brand listed twice. Five near-identical generic names — "Reptéri Parkolás", "Repteri Parkolas", "Reptéri Parkoló.com", "Reptér Parkoló", "Repteres Parkoló" — point at five different domains with no disambiguation. Safe Parking is placed at "Vecsés, Széchenyi u.", but Safe Parking's own site gives Üllői út 847 (postal address Almáskert út 15); Széchenyi u. 153/B is Parkolómester's address.

**6. No language information per operator** — despite the site itself being multilingual and its likely audience being exactly the Romanian, Serbian, Croatian and Slovak drivers who need to know which lots serve them. This is the field where a directory could add the most value and it is simply not collected.

**7. Coverage gaps.** It lists 33 entries; I identified ~45 distinct off-airport operators. Missing from it, among others: **Hispania** (4,145 reviews — by review volume the largest single lot at BUD), **MoPark** (1,572), **Airport Parking Hungary** (1,032), **Park & Bark** (1,292, and the only dog-boarding lot), **Pocket Parking** (the closest off-airport lot at 2.6 km, which its own "distance" filter would have highlighted), **ABC Gold Parking** (cheapest 4-day rate on the market), **Park & Rent**, **IQ Parking**, **ZENParking**, **Csendes Parkoló**, **PárnaPig**, **Null Terminál**, **Ferihegy Top**, **Star Park**, **Union**, **Hotel Ferihegy Parkoló** (cheapest single day at 4,990 Ft). Note it *does* list "ABC Parking" (abcparking.hu), which is not the same business as ABC Gold Parking.

**8. Filters that cannot fire.** The Type / Access / Features filters all render "0", and the counter reads "Show 0 lots" — the facet data is client-side only and does not appear to be populated for the listings.

### Bottom line

As a **narrative orientation guide** — how the airport is laid out, how to get there, what's nearby, what to watch for — airportparkingbudapest.info is decent and its 7-language build is genuinely ahead of the operators it covers. As a **price and location reference it should not be relied on**: no verifiable prices, an entry-price claim that doesn't match any real tariff, mostly estimated distances that are wrong at the low end, near-total absence of addresses and phone numbers, a missing official car park, apparent duplicates, and roughly a quarter of the actual market not listed. For live pricing, **ParkoLow** is the stronger comparison layer; for official zones, **parkolo.bud.hu** is the only authoritative source, since even the airport's own bud.hu information pages render prices client-side and publish no static tariff table.
